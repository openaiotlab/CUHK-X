02 SENSOR ARRAY

Every sample in CUHK-X is captured simultaneously by seven instruments. Three privacy-preserving streams ship in the public CUHK-S subset.

DEPTH

3-D structure from a Vzense NYX 650 — bodies as geometry, not identity.

● IN CUHK-S

INFRARED

Active IR video that keeps seeing when the lights go out.

● IN CUHK-S
